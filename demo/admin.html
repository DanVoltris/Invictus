Sellable bay-hours are the venue's opening hours for each day, minus closures painted on the tee sheet, minus time you blocked out yourself. Nothing here is an estimate — it is the same schedule the booking site sells from.

Prime time vs off-peak

—
A session that straddles the boundary is split by the minute, and its revenue is divided in the same proportion — so a 3–6pm booking puts two of its three hours, and two thirds of its money, in prime time.
